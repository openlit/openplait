# OpenPlait Brand

OpenPlait shares OpenLIT’s product visual language: stone surfaces, Inter, and
brand orange `#F36C06` — the same primary used on [openlit.io](https://openlit.io).

The mark represents several datasource streams braided into one portable
contract, then flowing back out to applications.

## Usage

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to one input-node diameter.
- Use the complete wordmark when the audience may not know OpenPlait.
- Use the mark alone for favicons, avatars, and compact navigation.
- Prefer white / stone planes with orange CTAs (same composition language as openlit.io).
- Do not rotate, outline, recolor individual paths, or place the logo on a busy image.
