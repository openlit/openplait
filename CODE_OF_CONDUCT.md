# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a welcoming, safe, and constructive project community.
Participants must communicate respectfully, assume good intent while focusing
on impact, accept technical feedback, and avoid harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks, or disclosure of private information.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation
when behavior threatens the community. Report conduct concerns privately to the
OpenLIT maintainers. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public spaces.
